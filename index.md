# CalGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

CalGo helps you view calendar events, create new events, add labels, and share event invitations.

## Information CalGo Accesses

CalGo may request access to:

- Calendar data, to display your events and create, update, or delete events you choose.
- Contacts, to let you select people to invite to an event.
- Location/map search, to help you choose an event location.
- Messages, to prepare SMS event invitations. Messages are only sent when you choose to send them.

CalGo does not sell your personal information.

## Event Data

Event information you create in CalGo may include the event name, description, location, date and time, recurrence, alerts, labels, and selected guests.

This data is saved to your device calendar through Apple EventKit. CalGo may store label information locally on your device so labels can be shown and filtered in the app.

## Advertising

CalGo uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may collect and use device identifiers, approximate location, app activity, and other data to provide, measure, and improve ads.

In regions where consent is required, CalGo uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request your advertising consent.

You can learn more about how Google uses data here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Sharing Invitations

When you invite guests, CalGo prepares a message with event details and an `.ics` calendar file. You review and send the message yourself. CalGo does not send messages automatically.

## Data Sharing

CalGo may share data with:

- Apple calendar services, when saving events to your calendar.
- Google AdMob, for advertising and consent management.
- Apple Messages, when you choose to send an invite.

## Data Retention

Calendar events are stored in your device calendar. Label information may be stored locally on your device. You can remove events or labels from within the app where available, or through your device calendar.

## Children’s Privacy

CalGo is not intended for children under 13.
